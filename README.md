# Iftiin Ascend

Build a flashy, modern, high-end marketing website for an online school called
"Iftiin Academy" ("Iftiin" is Somali for "light" — the brand promise is
"bringing knowledge and opportunity to the Somali diaspora, wherever they live").

TONE & DESIGN
- Premium, aspirational, energetic — think a mix of a modern edtech startup
  (Coursera/Skool-style polish) and a diaspora-community brand. NOT a generic
  Bootstrap template.
- Color palette: deep teal / indigo as the primary color, warm gold/amber as
  an accent (nods to Somali flag blue + a premium gold accent), off-white
  background, high contrast, generous whitespace.
- Bold, modern sans-serif headlines (large, confident type), clean body font.
- Smooth scroll animations, subtle hover effects on cards, sticky nav bar.
- Fully responsive — this site's #1 traffic source will be people scanning a
  QR code on a printed poster with their phone, so mobile layout must be
  flawless and fast-loading.

HERO SECTION
- Full-width hero with a large headline: "Iftiin Academy — Where the Somali
  Diaspora Learns, Grows, and Gets Ahead."
- Subheadline: "Language, careers, faith, and life skills — taught by people
  who understand your journey. Learn from anywhere, pay the way that works
  for you."
- Hero image: a warm, bright collage or single large photo-style illustration
  of diverse young diaspora learners (teens and adults) smiling, on laptops
  and phones, in a mix of casual and modest dress — NOT real people, use
  generated/stock-style imagery that reads as authentic and aspirational,
  not corporate stock-photo cheesy.
- Two prominent CTA buttons: "Browse Courses" and "Enroll Now / Join
  WhatsApp".
- Small trust strip under the hero: "Trusted by learners in the US, UK,
  Kenya & Somalia" with simple flag icons or a subtle world-map graphic.

WHY IFTIIN ACADEMY (short "edge" section)
- 3–4 icon + short-text cards:
  1. "Taught by our own" — courses built by Somali educators who know the
     culture and the language.
  2. "Real careers, real skills" — from trucking certifications to nursing
     prep, courses that lead to jobs.
  3. "Pay your way" — Stripe/PayPal for US members, EVC Plus/Zaad/M-Pesa via
     WhatsApp for Somalia & Kenya.
  4. "Learn on your schedule" — live classes plus downloadable lessons for
     low-bandwidth areas.

COURSE CATALOG — build this as the main content section, organized into
tabs or expandable groups by track. For EACH course below, generate:
 - a course card with a relevant, high-quality, tasteful generated image
   (never real people, never copyrighted logos/brands)
 - the course title
 - a punchy 1–2 sentence description written for a diaspora parent or young
   adult audience (aspirational, practical, warm tone — not corporate)
 - a small badge like "Flagship", "Popular", or "New"

Track 1 — Technology & Digital Skills
- Microsoft Office Essentials (Word, Excel, PowerPoint) — image: laptop with
  spreadsheet/doc on screen, warm workspace
- AI Tools for Beginners (ChatGPT, Claude, everyday AI use) — image: person
  using a phone/laptop with a friendly abstract "AI spark" visual
- Mathematics (school-level, exam-focused) — image: student writing at a
  desk with notebook and calculator
- Basic Coding / Web Design — image: colorful code editor on a screen
- Social Media & Digital Marketing Basics — image: phone showing a stylized
  social feed / content creation setup

Track 2 — Language & Culture (flagship track — feature prominently, larger
cards, "Flagship" badge)
- Somali Language for Diaspora Kids — image: bright, joyful classroom-style
  scene, kids with books, Somali-pattern decorative accents
- English Language (conversational & academic) — image: two learners in
  conversation, speech-bubble motif
- Quran, Arabic & Islamic Studies — image: calm, respectful study scene,
  open book, soft warm light (no depictions of faces in a way that feels
  disrespectful — keep tasteful and modest)

Track 3 — Trucking & Logistics (flagship track — feature prominently)
- CDL Permit Test Prep — image: modern semi-truck on an open highway
- Dispatch Fundamentals — image: dispatcher at a desk with monitors/maps
- Owner-Operator Basics — image: confident driver next to their truck,
  sunrise lighting

Track 4 — Immigration & Life-Abroad Prep
- US Citizenship / Naturalization Test Prep — image: passport/flag motif
- UK "Life in the UK" Test Prep — image: passport/flag motif (UK)
- Driving Theory Test Prep (US/UK/Kenya variants) — image: road sign /
  steering wheel study scene
- IELTS / TOEFL Prep — image: student writing an exam, headphones on

Track 5 — Career & Business Skills
- CV Writing & Job Interview Skills — image: confident person in an
  interview setting
- Small Business & Bookkeeping (Excel-based) — image: small shop owner with
  a ledger/laptop

Track 6 — School Support for Diaspora Kids
- Science (exam-focused) — image: bright science/lab-themed illustration
- English as a school subject — image: kids reading together

Track 7 — Family Life
- Parenting in the Diaspora (practical life-skills content only) — image:
  warm family scene, parent and child

Track 8 — High-Demand Additions
- Nursing / CNA / Healthcare Certification Prep — image: confident learner
  in scrubs, "Top Pick" badge
- Import-Export & Trade Business Skills — image: shipping containers /
  global trade motif
- Real Estate Investment for the Diaspora — image: modern home / property
  motif
- Halal / Islamic Finance & Investing — image: clean, professional finance
  chart motif
- Visa & Scholarship Guidance for Study Abroad — image: graduation cap /
  airport departure board motif

HOW IT WORKS / ENROLLMENT SECTION
- Simple 3-step visual: 1) Pick your course  2) Pay your way (Stripe/PayPal
  automatic for US, or send payment via EVC Plus/Zaad/M-Pesa and confirm on
  WhatsApp for Somalia/Kenya)  3) Get enrolled and start learning.
- Include a clear note: "Somalia & Kenya learners: after payment, message us
  your receipt on WhatsApp and we'll enroll you within hours."
- A prominent WhatsApp button (green, recognizable icon).

TESTIMONIALS / SOCIAL PROOF SECTION
- 3 placeholder testimonial cards with generated avatar-style images (not
  real people), a short quote, name, and location (e.g. "Amina, Minneapolis"
  / "Yusuf, Nairobi" / "Hodan, London"). Mark clearly in a code comment that
  these are placeholders to be replaced with real testimonials later.

MEET THE TEAM SECTION (optional, simple)
- Short cards for the founding team: Product & Tech Lead, Operations Lead,
  Curriculum Lead, Marketing Lead — use initials-based avatar placeholders,
  no real photos needed yet.

FOOTER
- Logo/name, short tagline, links (Courses, About, Contact, WhatsApp),
  social icons (TikTok, Facebook, Instagram), and a small line: "Pricing in
  USD-equivalent to protect against currency changes."

TECHNICAL NOTES
- This is a marketing/landing site to drive people to WhatsApp and to the
  actual course platform (Skool) — no login or payment processing needs to
  be built into this site itself; all "Enroll" buttons link out to WhatsApp
  or the Skool community link (use placeholder links I will swap in).
- Keep the whole site to a single polished landing page with clear anchor
  navigation (Home, Courses, How It Works, Testimonials, Contact) unless a
  natural case exists for a couple of extra pages.
- Fast load time is critical — optimize images, avoid heavy unused
  libraries.
